## Pilot scope

The Hypro builder now uses the proposed unified `cl-pack-engine.js` with Claude's `cl-pack-perso.js` adapter. Hypro is configured as `per_item` personalization with `preview.mode: "none"`; every selected pair carries its own required `Custom Text` value.

The GemPages-facing grid, configuration, and summary remain separable blocks. The pilot also includes the Hypro GemPages page shell on the unpublished theme so the complete page can be tested without touching production.

## Browser verification

Passed:

- Different products retain separate engraving text.
- The same variant can be selected more than once with different engraving text.
- Three selected pairs show a $149.97 subtotal, $50.97 savings, and $99.00 estimated total.
- Checkout receives three distinct lines and applies $33.00 per pair.
- One selected pair goes directly to Shopify Checkout at its regular $49.99 price.
- Returning from checkout restores the selected products, quantities, and text.
- Clear removes only the active Hypro bundle instance and resets the builder.
- The cart-page `Edit Hypro Sunglasses 3-Pack` link returns to the builder on one click and restores state.
- The header cart icon scrolls/opens the builder summary instead of opening the AMP drawer.
- Summary Edit reopens the correct Hypro card inline, hydrates its engraving, and changes the card CTA to Update.
- Saving an edit atomically replaces the personalized selection without changing its quantity; editing `EDIT ALPHA` to `EDIT BRAVO` left the pack count at one and removed the old value.
- Summary Remove deletes the exact personalized selection and resets the product card when its remaining aggregate quantity reaches zero.

Compatibility detail: `_bundle_size` now records the submitted bundle quantity, while `_bundle_max` records the offer ceiling. The deployed checkout validator therefore permits partial packs, and the backend discount remains authoritative for the three-pair reward.

Adapter pilot finding: an always-visible personalization block must remain visible after `reset()`. `cl-pack-perso.js` now hides the block only when the card actually has a personalization toggle.

Existing console noise from legacy discount-fetch and theme observer scripts was seen during testing; it was not generated by the unified engine and did not block the pilot flows.

## Merchant acceptance checklist

1. Open the preview link and confirm the preview bar says `Dev 8-10-2026` / `Draft`.
2. Add one pair with engraving and confirm direct checkout at $49.99.
3. Return to the builder and confirm the pair and engraving restore.
4. Add a second pair with different text and confirm direct checkout at $99.98.
5. Add a third pair and confirm checkout totals $99.00 ($33.00 each).
6. Test the same sunglasses twice with two different engraving values.
7. From the cart, click the Hypro edit link once and confirm it returns to the populated builder.
8. Click Clear and confirm only the active Hypro pack is removed.
9. Click the header cart icon on the builder and confirm it focuses the summary rather than opening AMP.
10. Click Edit beside a selected pair, change its engraving, and confirm Update replaces the text without increasing the quantity.
11. Click Remove and confirm only that personalized selection is removed.
